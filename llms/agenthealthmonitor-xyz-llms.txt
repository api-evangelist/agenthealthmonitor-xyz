# Agent Health Monitor (Digital Intensity Ltd)

> Trust and health verification for autonomous agents on Base L2 (eip155:8453). Fourteen pay-per-call REST endpoints — risk scores, wallet health reports, the 0–100 Agent Health Score (AHS) with A–F grades, tiered trust routing (instant_settle / escrow / reject), wash scans, gas optimisation, retry transactions, webhook alerts — paid per call in USDC over the x402 protocol or with a fiat X-API-Key bought via Stripe. A second service, AHM Verify, scores delivered agent output against a job spec ($0.50 per verdict, x402).

Generated by API Evangelist on 2026-09-19 from the provider's public surface (no /llms.txt is published on agenthealthmonitor.xyz, docs.agenthealthmonitor.xyz, blog., verify. or intelligence.). Independent third-party profile; not operated by the provider.

## APIs

- [Agent Health Monitor API](https://docs.agenthealthmonitor.xyz/): 74 paths / 76 operations, OpenAPI 3.1.0 v1.8.0. Base URL https://agenthealthmonitor.xyz. Paid routes answer HTTP 402 with a base64 PAYMENT-REQUIRED x402 v2 challenge; X-API-Key bypasses payment.
- [AHM Verify API](https://agenthealthmonitor.xyz/verify): POST /v1/specs (register a job spec, free), POST /v1/outputs (submit output, $0.50 USDC via x402), GET /v1/verdicts/{verdict_id} (poll). Base URL https://verify.agenthealthmonitor.xyz.

## Calling it

- Payment: x402 v2, USDC on Base (eip155:8453), facilitator https://facilitator.payai.network, or `X-API-Key: ahm_live_...` (Starter $9 = 100 calls, Pro $39 = 500 calls, Unlimited $99/month).
- Free previews: GET /retry/preview/{address}, GET /agent/protect/preview/{address}; free info: GET /api/info, GET /api/ecosystem-stats, GET /api/agent/{address}, GET /up.
- Errors: FastAPI `{"detail": ...}` envelope; 422 validation errors carry `detail[]` with loc/msg/type; 429 `{"error":"Rate limit exceeded","detail":"60 per 1 minute"}`; no rate-limit headers.
- Rate limits: per-IP 60/minute on most paid routes, 20/minute on /ahs/route/{address} and /report-card, 10/minute on /optimize and /retry/preview (from the provider's public source, not the docs).
- No idempotency keys, no pagination, no webhooks signing, no OAuth. Alerts: pay GET /alerts/subscribe/{address} (30 days), then POST /alerts/configure with webhook_url + webhook_type slack|discord|generic; DELETE /alerts/unsubscribe/{address} to stop.
